## Billing overview

Acme Cloud bills monthly on the first day of each calendar month. Usage is metered hourly across compute, storage, and egress. Invoices are issued to the account owner email and remain available in the console for seven years.

## Refund policy

Refunds are available within thirty days of the original charge. To request one, open a billing ticket from the console and include the invoice identifier. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within five business days.

## Rate limits

The public API permits one thousand requests per minute per project. Bursts above that ceiling receive a 429 response with a Retry-After header. Sustained overage for more than ten minutes triggers automatic throttling until the next billing hour begins, which protects shared infrastructure from noisy neighbours.

## Data residency

Customer data is stored in the region selected at project creation time. Acme Cloud operates regions in North America, the European Union, and Asia Pacific. Data never leaves the selected region except for aggregated anonymous telemetry.

## Support tiers

Standard support answers within one business day. Premium support answers within four hours and includes a named technical account manager who coordinates escalations across engineering teams during incidents.
